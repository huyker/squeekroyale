Gameplay
100 hamsters spawn empty-handed and loot 8 weapons themselves (Dagger · Sword · Axe · Spear · Hammer · Bow · Crossbow · Magic Staff).
Utility AI with 7 actions: ZONE · FLEE · ATTACK · LOOT · SUPPLY · HUNT · ROAM.
The Crown goes to the first to 3 kills; killing the king takes the Crown + restores 100% HP; a king killed by the zone drops the Crown.
The zone keeps shrinking; outside it is the Cat Storm (art only, no damage of its own).100 hamster spawn tay trắng, tự loot 8 vũ khí (Dagger · Sword · Axe · Spear · Hammer · Bow · Crossbow · Magic Staff).
Utility AI 7 hành động: ZONE · FLEE · ATTACK · LOOT · SUPPLY · HUNT · ROAM.
Crown ở 3 kill đầu tiên; giết vua → nhận Crown + hồi 100% HP; vua chết vì bo → Crown rơi xuống đất.
Vòng bo co dần, ngoài bo là Cat Storm (chỉ là art, không tự gây damage).
